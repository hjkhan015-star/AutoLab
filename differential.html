Differential. The engine (front) drives the propeller shaft, which turns the spiral-bevel pinion, which drives the crown wheel (ring gear) and its carrier. Driving straight, the spider gears ride along without spinning, so both axles turn at the same speed. In a turn the outer wheel travels further than the inner one — the spider gears now spin on their pins, letting the two side gears turn at different speeds.
