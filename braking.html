Brakes. Your foot applies a modest force on the pedal. A vacuum booster multiplies that ~4×. Inside the master cylinder, two small pistons pressurize the fluid — one for the front circuit (blue line, disc brakes) and one for the rear circuit (red line, drum brakes). Because pressure is equal everywhere (Pascal's law), the small MC piston acting on much larger caliper & wheel-cylinder pistons multiplies force again. Friction converts kinetic energy into heat — glowing rotors and drums.
Dual circuits are a safety feature: if one line ruptures, the other still brakes the vehicle.
