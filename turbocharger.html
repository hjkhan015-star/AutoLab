Turbocharger. Hot exhaust gas leaves the engine, dives down behind the block, and enters the turbine from below — spinning the turbine wheel. Through the shared shaft, the compressor pulls in fresh air: light sparse particles become a dense amber stream as they're compressed. The intercooler cools the charge before it reaches the intake manifold. The wastegate bleeds excess exhaust to cap boost; the blow-off valve vents the charge pipe when you lift off.
