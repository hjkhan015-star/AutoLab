Carburetor. Air rushes down through the venturi. As the throat narrows, the air speeds up and its pressure drops. Atmospheric pressure in the float chamber pushes fuel up through the main jet into the low-pressure throat, where it atomises and mixes with the airstream. The throttle valve below regulates how much mixture reaches the engine.
Idle circuit. With the throttle almost closed, only a trickle of air passes, but the idle port still draws enough fuel to keep the engine running smoothly — the mixture runs deliberately rich.
