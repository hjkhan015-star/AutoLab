Clutch. The flywheel spins with the engine. The friction disc is splined to the gearbox input shaft and squeezed between the flywheel and the pressure plate by a diaphragm spring. Releasing the pedal lets the spring clamp the disc. Pressing the pedal pushes the throw-out bearing into the diaphragm fingers, releasing the clamp force so the disc slips freely.
Speed ~800 rpm
