Fuel Injection (MPFI). An electric pump pressurizes a common fuel rail. Each cylinder's injector is commanded by the ECU to open for a precise number of milliseconds — spraying a fine mist directly at the intake valve. The O₂ sensor in the exhaust measures leftover oxygen, and the ECU constantly trims injection time to hold the mixture near the ideal 14.7:1 ratio. This closed-loop control system keeps the engine running cleanly and efficiently.
